## Prompt (English)

```text
tell it to act as an architectural visualization agency that handles lead generation, sample creation, outreach preparation, and 3D production.

give it these instructions:

lead generation

* use Apify Google Maps Scraper to find businesses in your chosen niche.
* crawl their websites for floor plans and architectural renders.
* identify businesses without interactive 3D tours.
* collect their plans, photos, and public business contacts.
* organize everything in a spreadsheet.

sample creation

* select the best floor plan from each business.
* generate three photorealistic room samples using GPT Image 2.5 or a short Seedance walkthrough.
* compare the results against the business’s existing property photos.
* prepare a personalized outreach email for each prospect.
* show you the samples and drafted emails for approval before sending anything.

3D production

once a client pays:

* convert the floor plan into structured data for walls, rooms, doors, and windows.
* use Blender Python to build the 3D environment.
* use Three.js to create the interactive browser experience.
* add furniture, textures, lighting, and realistic materials.
* include upgrade options buyers can explore inside each room.
* add pricing and lead capture to the experience.
* optimize the final walkthrough for mobile.

quality control

use a separate AI critic to inspect every room against the client’s original photos.

it identifies problems, suggests measurable fixes, and reviews the updated renders until they meet the required standard.

your final deliverables:

→ a spreadsheet of qualified leads
→ personalized samples and drafted emails
→ a hosted, interactive 3D walkthrough for every paying client
→ room-by-room renders and quality reports
```
