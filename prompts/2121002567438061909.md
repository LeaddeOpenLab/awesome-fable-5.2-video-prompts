## Prompt (English)

```text
# Task: model Razor's Ford Mustang GT (NFS Most Wanted) in Blender, matching my reference images

You are working in a live Blender session through the Blender MCP tools. Build a 3D model of a 2005 Ford Mustang GT (S197) with a body kit and a black-with-flames livery.

A previous attempt failed because the body was shaped by eye: it came out as a generic coupe with a wide, boxy, Corvette-like rear, a wrong roofline and no Mustang character. So the rule for this attempt is: the body is traced from the reference images, not invented, and you may not move on to details until the silhouette provably matches.

The references (on disk, also attached here)
E:\Claude\CARS\reference_4view.jpg — a 2x2 grid of photos of the target car: front (top left), rear (top right), side with the nose pointing right (bottom left), side with the nose pointing left (bottom right).
E:\Claude\CARS\reference_top.jpg — the same car seen from high above the front. It shows the hood, the hood flames, the roof scoop, the wing and how the body narrows in plan.

These two images are the only references and the source of truth for everything: body shape, kit, wheels, livery. The final model should look like this car from every one of these angles.

The photos have perspective, so they are not exact orthographic drawings. Anchor the scale with the real dimensions of the car and use the photos for the shapes:
Length 4.765 m, width 1.877 m plus the flared arches, height about 1.35 m (the car is lowered)
Wheelbase 2.720 m, front and rear track about 1.60 m
Wheels about 19 inch with low-profile tires

Stage 1: set up the references in the viewport
Start from an empty scene. Check https://t.co/fdimPQS8xI.version and use the matching API. Work in meters, car centered on the origin, wheels resting on Z=0.

Crop the 4-view image into its four panels and load them as reference image empties: the side photo in the side orthographic view, the front and rear photos in the front and rear views. Scale and position the side photo so the wheel centers are 2.720 m apart and the tires sit on Z=0; scale the front and rear photos so the body height matches the side view. The top photo is taken at an angle, so don't trace it as a top view; use it by eye for the plan shape and for the hood, roof and wing.

Stage 2: trace the body
Build the body mesh by placing vertices on the outlines in the photos: first the side profile, then the front and rear sections. For the plan shape use the top photo and the known width: the body is widest at the wheel arches, and the cabin (greenhouse) is clearly narrower than the body, with visible shoulders either side of it. Use a mirror modifier and subdivision surface with supporting edge loops.

The features that make it an S197, all of which the last attempt missed:
Long flat hood, short rear deck, cabin set well back
Fastback roofline that falls in one sweep into a short trunk lid
Upright, slightly forward-leaning nose with a wide rectangular grille
A sharp shoulder line running the full length of the body
The C-shaped scoop pressed into the side behind the door
Small triangular rear quarter window
A rear that is not wider than the front; flat vertical tail panel with a recessed area between the taillights

Stage 3: verification gate
Take orthographic viewport screenshots from the side, front and rear with the reference photo visible behind a semi-transparent model, plus one screenshot from the same high front angle as the top photo. Compare the outlines: roofline, hood line, beltline, window openings, wheel arch positions, front and rear overhangs, cabin width against body width. List every deviation you see and fix it. Repeat until the model's outline sits on the photo outlines. Do not start on the kit, wheels or materials before this passes, and show me the overlay screenshots when it does.

Stage 4: body kit
Hood: raised cowl-style center bulge with two long ridges and two vent openings facing the windshield.
Roof: a low, wide, rounded-rectangle scoop centered at the front of the roof, opening forward.
Front: wide black grille with a chrome running-pony emblem in the center; a rectangular headlight at each outer end and two round auxiliary lamps set inside the grille; a deep bumper with a large black mesh lower intake and a splitter lip; two vertical brushed-metal bars running from the round lamps down the bumper face to the splitter.
Sides: mildly flared arches, deep side skirts, three vertical vent slits on the front fender just behind the wheel arch.
Rear: three-segment red taillights on each side, a round GT emblem centered between them, a third brake light at the top of the trunk lid, license plate reading "ND4 SPD", two vertical black bars on the bumper either side of the plate, two oval exhaust outlets in the lower bumper.
Wing: a tall GT wing, nearly as wide as the car, with a curved blade, two supports and large angular end plates. It needs real thickness and an airfoil section, not a thin rod.
Wheels: silver 12-spoke, filling the arches. The car sits low, and the tires touch the ground.
Also: side mirrors, door handles, wipers, panel gaps, lightly tinted glass with a simple dark interior visible behind it (seats, dashboard, steering wheel).

Stage 5: materials and livery
Base: deep gloss black paint with a clear coat.

Flames, copied from the references as closely as you can: sharp-edged classic hot-rod flames with curling tips and black cutouts inside, colored pale yellow at the origin, through orange, to red at the tips.
Hood (see the top photo): two separate flame groups, one on each side of the center bulge. Each starts as a solid yellow base at the front edge of the hood, directly above a round auxiliary lamp and in line with the metal bar below it, and rises toward the windshield in four or five licking tongues that end about two thirds of the way up the hood. The bulge and the center of the hood stay black.
Each side (see the side photos): the flame starts solid yellow on the front fender behind the wheel arch, sweeps back across the door and ends in long thin red tips on the rear quarter panel. A small white skull-like emblem sits inside the flame on the door, near the handle.

Draw the flames as bezier curve shapes traced over the reference photos, bake them to a 4K mask per projection (side, top), and project the masks onto the body. Edges must be crisp. No blurry gradients, no stretching, no flames smeared over the wheel arches or bumper. The previous attempt produced soft orange blobs; that is not acceptable.

Other materials: silver metallic wheels, rubber tires, red taillight lenses, clear headlight glass over reflectors, black mesh grilles, brushed metal for the two bumper bars.

Scene and renders
The scene contains the car and nothing else: no ground plane, no water or puddles, no track, no props, no backdrop. Light it with a neutral HDRI or studio lights so the paint has clean reflections, but render with a transparent background (Film > Transparent), so the HDRI is not visible in the image.

Render in Cycles at 1920x1080 as PNG with alpha: the four views of reference_4view.jpg (front, rear, both sides), the high front view of reference_top.jpg, and one front three-quarter view.

How to work
Send code in small chunks so a failure doesn't lose everything.
After every stage, take screenshots, compare against the references, list the differences and fix them before continuing.
Save to project folder after each stage.
Finish with a short honest report: what matches, what still deviates.
```
